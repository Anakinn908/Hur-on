November Senior Activity Workshops

Age is just a number — there is always something new to learn, create and enjoy.

A series of fun, engaging activities to keep our seniors active, creative and socially connected. Whether it is exploring creativity, enjoying a friendly game or simply making new connections, there is something for everyone.

What’s in store

  • Board GamesLight-hearted, socially engaging tabletop games that encourage conversation and thinking.
  • Mandala ColouringSymmetry, shading and patterning to relieve stress and engage both sides of the brain.
  • Paper Mosaic ArtPiece patterned card stock into an intricate design, building spatial logic and hand-eye coordination.
  • Dot PaintingA focused, therapeutic acrylic dotting class that builds precision and patience.

A practical approach

Our programmes combine relevant knowledge, facilitated discussion, reflection and simple actions participants can apply in daily life.

BLOOM focuses on education, skills and personal development. It is not a substitute for clinical mental-health treatment or medical care.
