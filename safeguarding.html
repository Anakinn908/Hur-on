Safeguarding

Keeping people safe.

Our commitment to the safety of everyone in our programmes.

Our commitment

Every child, young person and adult who takes part in a HUR-ON programme has the right to be safe and treated with dignity. Where children and young people are involved — including our SPARK programmes, camps and events — their welfare comes first.

Our child-protection principles:

  • the welfare of the child is the first consideration in every decision;
  • every child has the right to be protected from harm, abuse, neglect and exploitation, whatever their age, ability, background or belief;
  • every concern is taken seriously and acted on promptly;
  • safeguarding is everyone’s responsibility, and no one is expected to deal with a concern alone.

Who this applies to

Everyone who works with or for HUR-ON: our team, facilitators, contractors, volunteers and delivery partners.
